## Verdict

**No CRITICAL or HIGH finding: 3 MEDIUM, 8 LOW, all in the two rewritten modules.** The P1 and HIGH 6 fixes hold:
nothing was found in their diffs.

**What holds:**
- Myslot's codec is linear and bounded:
  - every read stops at the end of its message;
  - nesting stops at depth 4, a varint at 10 bytes;
  - a malformed text raises an error inside a `pcall` (`Myslot/Myslot.lua:238`);
  - the bench checks it writes the same bytes as lua-pb.
- Every Myslot change of bars, macros, bindings and click bindings runs in a click, out of combat. Combat is checked
  at the click and again at the Yes (`Tab.lua:131, 146, 169, 379`). Rule 7 holds, and with `share.lua` gone no input
  comes from other players.
- DoesItDie never compares a secret value in Lua: health goes into StatusBars and curves only, and every value it
  reads goes through `isSecret` first.
- DoesItDie registers its events only while it is on (`DoesItDie.lua:1293-1312`). Its single driver sleeps when
  nothing is tracked: 0 CPU at rest.
- The rule 7 moves are in the clicks, as described:
  - the announce lists (`W.AnnounceLines`);
  - the group mover (`RG.ApplyGroups`);
  - the raid conversion (`Pump`);
  - the Loot Council award line (`LC.AskAward`).
- `Held` and the 60 s whisper window close the master audit's HIGH 1.
- `select(6, ...)` in `PARTY_INVITE_REQUEST` is `inviterGUID` in Forever's payload [V-doc].

**The weak spots:**
1. **DoesItDie, switched on in a group, pays for the whole raid instead of the player's DoTs:**
   - every friendly unit's `UNIT_COMBAT` runs through its tracker (M1);
   - every raider's hit on a mob carrying a DoT writes a trace line (M2);
   - the 10 Hz pass reads all 40 nameplate tokens (M3).
   Together, in a 40-man pull: about 0.05-0.17 ms per frame at 60 FPS (estimate), plus about 50-100 KB/s of
   short-lived tables and strings. The module is off by default; off, it costs nothing.
2. **M1 also breaks one of the module's own rules in groups:** "errs low, never a false kill".
3. **Myslot's restore trusts the counts and ranges of a pasted text** (L4).
4. **The master audit's PvP FPS list (P3) is still open**: the BG bench shows it unchanged.

## MEDIUM

**M1. DoesItDie: the `UNIT_COMBAT` of every unit token runs through the tracker, and friendly events erase the
mob's last outcome. [V, M; the raid tokens PLAUSIBLE]**
- Where: `DoesItDie/DoesItDie.lua`:
  - l.1241 and 1298: `RegisterEvent("UNIT_COMBAT")`, for every unit;
  - l.977-990: `onUnitCombat`;
  - l.617-623: `onHit`; l.589-591: `onAvoided`.
- What runs when a raider takes a hit (WOUND) and nothing is tracked, before `dotsByTarget[key]` (l.989) says there
  is nothing to do:
  - `W.Measure` and `ns.Allowed()`;
  - 5 `isSecret` pcalls and `pcall(UnitGUID)`;
  - a new `lastOutcome` table, about 220 bytes (`onHit`, l.620).
  A raider's dodge, parry or miss goes to `onAvoided`, which replaces `lastOutcome` too (l.591).
- Measured (fengari, 4,000 events, against the module off):
  - +0.068 ms per event on raid2..40;
  - +0.070 ms per event on mobs without a DoT.
- In a 40-man boss pull (estimate: 200-400 events per second, from raiders taking damage plus 2-4 tokens per raider
  hit on the boss):
  - 14-27 ms/s fengari, about 1.2-5.4 ms/s native;
  - about 20-40 KB/s of `lastOutcome` tables for the friendly events alone.
- Accuracy:
  - `avoidJustBefore` (l.534-540) only looks at the one `lastOutcome`.
  - A mob's dodge, parry or miss that comes just before the cast event (the 0.25 s look-back, l.400) is lost as soon
    as any friendly unit is hit in between. In a raid, that is nearly always.
  - The avoided DoT then counts at once (`waitFirstTick` is "off" by default, l.179). It stays until 2 tick slots
    pass without a tick (`MISSED_TICKS_BEFORE_DROP`, l.52): about 6.5 s for a 3 s DoT.
  - During that time a kill icon can show for a DoT that is not on the mob. The module's rule says the opposite:
    "errs low (no false skull)" (l.386-388), "never a false kill" (l.397).
- Fix:
  - At the top of the `UNIT_COMBAT` branch, before any `pcall`, drop every token that cannot be an enemy mob.
  - Use a set built once: `target`, `focus`, `softenemy`, `mouseover`, `nameplate1` to `nameplate40` (the tokens
    `unitKey` can key).
  - A `bossN` token adds nothing: in an instance its GUID is secret and it has no plate key of its own; in the open
    world the same hit also comes on the target or plate token.
  - `lastOutcome` then only ever holds mob outcomes.
- Gain:
  - the friendly half of the traffic costs one table lookup instead of about 0.068 ms fengari;
  - about 0.6-2.7 ms/s native, and no `lastOutcome` garbage for that half;
  - the look-back works in groups again.
- To check in game: `/wl perf` after a raid pull with the module on, before and after the fix: the count and the total
  of "DoesItDie events DoesItDie.lua:1376" (1.41.55), then of `DoesItDie.lua:1476` (after fix 1).

## Recommendations of the brief not taken (Lua 5.1 and Forever facts)

- **Cache every global as a file-level local** (`local pairs = pairs`).
  - In Lua 5.1 a global read is one hash lookup in the function's environment (`GETGLOBAL`), against an upvalue read
    (`GETUPVAL`). That matters only in tight Lua loops.
  - None of the hot paths measured here is bound by it: they are bound by API calls, pcalls and new tables.
  - Each cached name is one more upvalue in every closure that uses it, and more locals in the file (see the limits
    above).
  - So: only in a loop that a measurement shows is bound by globals.
- **`string.format` instead of `..`.**
  - In Lua 5.1, `a .. b .. c` is one `OP_CONCAT` over consecutive registers: one new string, no intermediate one.
  - `string.format` parses its pattern on top of that.
  - The garbage comes from strings built for nothing (M2's HIT lines, L8's hidden label, the signature string per
    `UNIT_COMBAT` at `DoesItDie.lua:998`), not from the operator.
- **Bit-pack the addon messages.**
  - The messages are already short fields (`W.Pack`), and texts over 300 characters go deflated (`W.SendLong`).
  - Base64 adds a third, so packing a short message makes it longer.
  - The rewritten code sends nothing.
- **Filter the combat log (CLEU).** Forever refuses `COMBAT_LOG_EVENT_UNFILTERED` to addons (ADDON_ACTION_FORBIDDEN:
  `PvP/Main.lua:962`, `DoesItDie.lua:1249`). WhoPulled registers it only off Forever (`WhoPulled.lua:54, 70`:
  `CLEU_OK = not W.FOREVER`).
- **`UNIT_AURA` for tracked units only.** Already so for AuraTracker (`RegisterUnitEvent` per unit) and Skyborne (the
  player). PvP Dispel (every unit, in battlegrounds) is the master audit's 33.
- **`pcall` where strictly needed.** DoesItDie's pcalls sit where Forever can error or hand out a secret (Blizzard's
  frames, unit reads). The cost is M1: they run for units that need none.
